RESEARCH UCP PORTO

🏛️ Welcome to XR Heritage Framework

This visual studio is part of a doctoral research project at the School of Arts, Universidade Católica Portuguesa (UCP Porto) by Manuel Silva (supervised by Prof. Luís Teixeira). It provides a validated bridge between AI-assisted creative mediation, curatorial autonomy, and XR game-engine implementation.

1 Co-Design with the AI Assistant

Copy our Master AI Prompt and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity. The AI will first ask for your role (Artist or Curator) to adapt its language, protect your creative voice, and produce your experience design.

📄 View on GitHub

2 Visualize & Generate PDF Dossier

Paste the AI's JSON output directly into this Studio. Preview the 3D gallery floorplan maquette and interactive viewport, attach your visual renders, and click Save Visual Report (PDF) for your exhibition team.

3 Developer Handoff or Gentle Engine On-Ramp

Click Export Unity .JSON to send to your developer. Or if you want to try testing it yourself in Unity, our guide will walk you through a gentle 3-step no-code walkthrough.

📦 Install Unity Package (UPM)

Add the package via Git URL in Unity Package Manager (Unity 2021.3 LTS+):

https://github.com/LeunamAvlis/XR-Heritage-Framework-Toolkit.git?path=/Unity_Package

🤖 Modular AI Skills & Antigravity

For CLI / Antigravity / Agentic developers, all 4 modular AI skills are located in AI_Skills/. You can run individual agents for profiling, affordance matchmaking, and TAM generation.

🎨 1-Click Experience Setup Wizard

Open Tools > XR Heritage Framework > Experience Setup Wizard in Unity. Importing the artist's JSON automatically installs AR/VR SDKs, builds the XR Rig, instantiates 3D Hotspot pins, and wires the UI canvas.

🔬 Doctoral Research Context

Developed at School of Arts, Portuguese Catholic University (UCP Porto). Built on an empirical benchmark of 80+ Worldwide museum XR projects (2005–2024) and evaluated by panels of curators, artists, and creative technologists.

📊 Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) Integration

The framework automatically embeds validated in-situ micro-surveys measuring Perceived Usefulness (PU), Ease of Use (PEOU), Curatorial Respect (CR), and Artist Autonomy Preservation (AAP), logging anonymous visitor data to CSV.
