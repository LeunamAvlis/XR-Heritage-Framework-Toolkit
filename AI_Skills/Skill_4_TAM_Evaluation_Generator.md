# Skill 4: TAM Evaluation & Research Analytics Generator
## Purpose
To automatically synthesize customized, validated evaluation questionnaires and research instruments based on the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), enabling rigorous qualitative and quantitative data collection.

---

### Standard 5-Dimension TAM Heritage Framework

Each questionnaire is structured into five standard scientific dimensions scored on a 5-point or 7-point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree / Completely False, 5 = Strongly Agree / Completely True):

#### 1. Perceived Usefulness (PU) - Weight: 25%
- *"The XR experience helped me understand aspects of the artwork that were not visible in the physical display."*
- *"The technological mediation added genuine educational and cultural value to my museum visit."*
- *"Comparing the artist's preparatory sketches with the final piece deepened my appreciation of the artwork."*

#### 2. Perceived Ease of Use (PEOU) - Weight: 20%
- *"Navigating between the artist and curator perspectives was intuitive and required no prior training."*
- *"The device was comfortable to hold and did not obstruct my viewing of the authentic artwork."*
- *"The interface responses and touch/gesture targets were responsive and clear."*

#### 3. Content Relevance & Curatorial Respect (CR) - Weight: 25%
- *"The digital content felt respectful of the artist's work and did not diminish the museum atmosphere."*
- *"The balance between curatorial context and the artist's personal voice was well proportioned."*
- *"The information provided was historically and aesthetically credible."*

#### 4. Facilitation of Interaction & Engagement (FIE) - Weight: 15%
- *"The experience encouraged me to spend more time observing details on the original physical artwork."*
- *"The interactive hotspots allowed me to direct my own curiosity rather than being forced through a rigid tour."*

#### 5. Behavioral Intention & Recommendation (BI) - Weight: 15%
- *"I would like to see similar XR experiences accompanying other exhibitions in this museum."*
- *"I would recommend this interactive experience to friends and family visiting the museum."*
