# Skill 2: Multi-Perspective Content Mediation & Co-Design
## Purpose
To structure the conceptual content of the XR experience so that it respects the artist's autonomy, fulfills curatorial educational goals, and avoids distracting or disrespectful technological gimmicks.

---

### The 3-Layer Content Architecture

Every artwork mediated through the framework is deconstructed into three complementary layers:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    PHYSICAL ARTWORK LAYER                       │
│  - Primacy of the authentic physical object                     │
│  - Visual silence: no intrusive 3D clutter obscuring the canvas │
└───────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────┘
                                │
        ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
        ▼                                               ▼
┌───────────────────────────────┐       ┌───────────────────────────────┐
│      LAYER A: ARTIST VOICE    │       │     LAYER B: CURATORIAL LENS   │
│  "The Creative Genesis"       │       │  "Context & Significance"     │
├───────────────────────────────┤       ├───────────────────────────────┤
│ • Artist's own narrative      │       │ • Art historical context      │
│ • Evolution from sketches     │       │ • Formal & technical analysis │
│ • Discarded drafts & doubts   │       │ • Dialogue with other works   │
│ • Emotional / poetic intent   │       │ • Sociopolitical relevance    │
│ • Connection to artist's body │       │ • Educational interpretation  │
│   of work                     │       │   for varied demographics     │
└───────────────────────────────┘       └───────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### The "Meaningful Mediation vs. Gimmick" Filter

Before validating any proposed XR interaction, evaluate it against the following litmus test:

| Proposed Feature | Is it a Gimmick? ❌ | Is it Meaningful Mediation? ✅ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Animation on Painting** | Animating static characters in a classical painting like a cartoon. | Revealing the underdrawing (pentimento) or infrared reflectography layer through an interactive slider. |
| **3D Floating Elements** | Random 3D particles or sparkling effects hovering in front of an artwork. | Placing a 3D architectural reconstruction in an adjacent virtual window or viewing corridor. |
| **Audio Overlay** | Loud cinematic background music playing continuously. | Spatialized voiceover of the artist explaining a specific detail, audible only when focusing on that segment. |
| **Gamification** | Points/score system for looking at artworks quickly. | Exploratory discovery of hidden compositional geometry or pigment origin through intuitive touch lenses. |

---

### Artist Autonomy Protection Clause

When negotiating with curators and technical developers, the framework enforces the **Artist Charter**:
1. **Consent on Augmentations**: The artist has veto power over any digital asset placed within the direct field of view of their physical work.
2. **Attribution & Voice**: Artist reflections must use authentic archival quotes, recordings, or approved texts.
3. **Reversibility**: Visitors must always be able to easily lower the device or tap a single "Clear View" button to return to uninterrupted contemplative viewing of the real artwork.
