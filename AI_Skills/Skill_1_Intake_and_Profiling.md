# Skill 1: Intake & Context Profiling
## Purpose
To eliminate premature assumptions and guarantee that any XR proposal is firmly rooted in the physical artwork, the exhibition space, and the specific stakeholder role.

---

### Step-by-Step Intake Form

When starting a new project consultation, present this concise diagnostic template:

```markdown
### 🏛️ XR Heritage Intake Diagnostic

Please provide details for the following 5 parameters (or answer what you have ready):

1. **Your Role**: [Curator / Artist / XR Designer / Museum Educator / Academic Researcher]
2. **Artwork Identity & Medium**:
   - Title & Artist (if public or known):
   - Medium: [e.g., Oil painting, Photography series, Bronze sculpture, Archaeological fragment, Site-specific installation]
   - Physical dimensions & conservation limits: [e.g., Light-sensitive, enclosed in glass case, touchable replica available]
3. **Exhibition Setting**:
   - Space: [White cube gallery, historic heritage building, outdoor monument, church/cathedral]
   - Lighting & Acoustics: [Low ambient lighting, daylight, reverberant hall, quiet room]
   - Visitor Flow: [High turnover / crowded, contemplative / slow-paced]
4. **Primary Curatorial / Artistic Goal**:
   - What key story or dimension *cannot* be communicated by the physical artwork alone?
   - [e.g., Reveal preparatory drafts, explain complex fabrication techniques, contextualize historical provenance, multi-sensory accessibility]
5. **Hardware Constraints**:
   - [BYOD (Visitor smartphones) / Dedicated Museum Tablets / Standalone VR Headsets (Quest 3) / Projection Mapping / WebXR kiosk]
```

---

### Adaptive Probing Logic

Depending on the stakeholder role, trigger specialized questions:

#### If the user is an **Artist**:
- *"What aspects of your creative process (sketches, discarded ideas, underlying thoughts) are invisible in the final work that you would like visitors to discover?"*
- *"Are there strict boundaries on what digital alterations or overlays should NEVER be done to your artwork?"*

#### If the user is a **Curator**:
- *"How does this artwork relate to the broader exhibition narrative or thematic groupings?"*
- *"What balance of mediation vs. contemplative viewing do you wish to maintain (i.e., avoiding visitor distraction from the physical object)?"*

#### If the user is an **XR Developer / Designer**:
- *"What tracking constraints exist in the space (e.g., reflections on glass cases, uniform flat walls, fluctuating sunlight)?"*
- *"What is the target session duration per visitor (e.g., 90 seconds in-situ vs. 10 minutes seated VR)?"*
